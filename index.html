About Me

Sai Puppala is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southern Illinois University Carbondale, working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and cybersecurity. His research focuses on developing robust, adaptive, and secure intelligent systems addressing contemporary challenges in cybersecurity and data integrity, particularly in dynamic and resource-constrained environments. He is a member of the Security and Privacy Enhanced Machine Learning (SUPREME) Lab, advised by Dr. Sajedul Talukder and Dr. Koushik Sinha.

As a Senior Research Software Engineer at John Deere, Sai applies his expertise in Big Data, Machine Learning, and Cloud Services to solve complex problems across Manufacturing, Transportation, and Healthcare domains. He has authored over 40 peer-reviewed publications in prestigious journals and top-tier conferences including IEEE COMPSAC, IEEE/ACM ASONAM, ICWSM, and the American Nuclear Society.

Research Interests

  • Federated Learning — secure, communication-efficient, and adaptive federated learning for resource-constrained and 6G edge environments
  • Agentic AI — autonomous AI systems capable of planning, tool use, and multi-step reasoning to accomplish complex real-world tasks
  • Healthcare AI — predictive analytics, wearable technology, and federated learning for patient privacy
  • Cybersecurity — quantum-inspired models, anomaly detection, and proactive threat intelligence for critical infrastructure
  • Generative AI & LLMs — GPT-based systems, RAG pipelines, and multimodal summarization
